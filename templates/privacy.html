Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Data We Collect

Spendly collects the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, along with the expense data you choose to log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the expense tracking service: to display your spending history, generate summaries and breakdowns, and keep your account secure. We do not use your financial data for advertising.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use trusted third-party providers for hosting and infrastructure, who are bound to handle data only on our behalf.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
